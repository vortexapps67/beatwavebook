Reader Privacy Policy

How Vortex Apps protects your personal information, purchase records, and reading privacy.

1. Introduction & Commitment to Privacy

Welcome to The Story of BeatWave (Vortex Apps). We deeply respect your privacy and are committed to maintaining the highest ethical standards regarding data transparency. As an independent, open-source-aligned publishing project, we believe in collecting only the bare minimum information required to deliver your book, process your payment verification, and provide customer support.

Key Guarantee: We never sell, rent, monetize, or disclose your personal data, email addresses, phone numbers, or reading activity to data brokers, ad networks, or marketing intermediaries.

2. Information We Collect

When you interact with our website, create a reader account, or place a book order, we may collect the following information:

  • Personal Identity & Contact Details: Your full name, email address, and WhatsApp/phone number provided during account creation or checkout.
  • Delivery Address: Physical postal address, pin code, and state (only when ordering physical paperback collector editions).
  • UPI Payment Reference: Sender UPI ID / VPA or payment screenshot transaction handle (e.g., akshansh@axl) to verify peer-to-peer payment clearance.
  • Account Credentials: Password entered during reader account creation, securely synchronized with our backend so you can log in, track shipments, and access your digital library.
  • Reading & Device State: Bookmark progress and active chapter states saved in local device storage (localStorage) to resume your reading journey.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used strictly for legitimate book fulfillment and reader communication purposes:

  • To verify your UPI transaction with our banking ledger and generate your order confirmation invoice.
  • To dispatch the unabridged Digital E-Book (PDF/EPUB) edition directly to your reader profile and email.
  • To print shipping waybills and coordinate physical courier delivery for paperback collector copies.
  • To provide timely order status notifications via WhatsApp and email (Order Received → Payment Verified → Dispatched).
  • To provide reader support, handle errata reports, and resolve any fulfillment inquiries.

4. UPI Payment Security (NPCI Direct Transfers)

We process orders through direct peer-to-peer UPI (Unified Payments Interface) managed by the National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) through authorized banking apps (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, Cred):

  • We never collect or store sensitive banking credentials: We have no access to your UPI PIN, bank account passwords, debit/credit card numbers, or CVV codes.
  • All monetary transactions occur entirely within your own bank-grade UPI application.
  • We only receive your public transaction identifier and sender name to reconcile incoming credits against your generated BW-XXXXXX order reference.

5. Database Infrastructure & Cloud Security

Our backend is powered by Supabase (PostgreSQL enterprise cloud architecture) with active Row-Level Security (RLS), SSL/TLS end-to-end transport encryption, and isolated author-access controls.

Access to live order ledgers is restricted strictly to verified authors (Akshansh, Aarav, and the Vortex core maintainer team) via cryptographic role-based authorization.

6. Cookies & Local Storage

We do not use third-party tracking cookies, Google Analytics invasive remarketing pixels, or Facebook ad beacons. We solely utilize essential client-side web storage (localStorage / sessionStorage) to maintain:

  • Your active reader login session.
  • Your pending cart items.
  • Your selected dark/light ambient visual preferences.

7. Data Retention & Your Deletion Rights

You retain complete control over your personal data. At any time, you have the right to:

  • Access Your Records: Request a full copy of all order entries, addresses, and account logs associated with your email.
  • Account & Data Deletion: Request permanent purging of your reader account and profile from our Supabase database.
  • Rectification: Update your delivery address or contact number prior to physical shipment dispatch.

To exercise any of these rights, email our Data Protection Officer at beatwave790@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Privacy Request". Requests are executed within 48 business hours.
